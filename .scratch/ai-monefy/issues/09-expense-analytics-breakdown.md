# 09: Expense Analytics & Spending Breakdown

**What to build:** A dedicated analytics dashboard presenting category rankings by total spend, expense share percentages, and visual comparison bars.

**Blocked by:** 03: Circular Donut Wheel & Balance Hub, 05: Time Interval Navigation & Period Filtering

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] An analytics view lists all expense categories ranked from highest spending to lowest for the selected time interval.
- [ ] Each category row displays the monetary total, percentage of total expenses, and a proportional horizontal visual bar.
- [ ] Tapping a category row shows the list of individual transactions contributing to that total.

# 06: Multi-Account Management & Account Transfers

**What to build:** Multi-account support (Cash, Payment Card, Savings) allowing users to isolate their view to a single account or see all accounts combined, plus an account transfer workflow.

**Blocked by:** 03: Circular Donut Wheel & Balance Hub

**Status:** done

- [x] User can view a breakdown of balances across all registered accounts.
- [x] Selecting a specific account filters the wheel and transaction totals to that account only; selecting "All" displays unified aggregates.
- [x] Transferring funds from one account to another (e.g. Card to Cash) adjusts both account balances without altering overall net income or expense totals.
- [x] User can add, rename, or archive financial accounts.
